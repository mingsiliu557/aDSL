Initial print grouping. Semantic parts need not be separate print parts; one print part may contain several semantic parts. Separating a part can add seams, interface geometry, assembly work and failure risk. Keep small attached features with their parent body by default, unless a specific print-orientation or support benefit, an explicit task requirement, or an assembly need justifies separation. Check that the intended contact region has plausible space for the chosen interface. A small feature may still be separated when that benefit or requirement justifies a feasible connection. Separate print parts require valid connections, and the resulting assembly must satisfy the configured topology and standing checks where applicable. Explain each initial print-group choice in relations, including why attached features are grouped or separated. These reasons are hypotheses, not measured gains. Preserve explicitly requested detachable parts. If require_multiple_parts is true, it requires at least two print parts and one connector, not a separate print part for every semantic feature.
